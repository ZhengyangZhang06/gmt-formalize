Notation: G := Grassmannian N n (compact, metrizable, second countable: SimonGMT Grassmannian.instCompactSpace / instSecondCountableTopology), Z := ℝ^N × G (locally compact, Hausdorff, second countable), C(Z, ℝ) with the compact-open topology.

1. (C(Z, ℝ) is second countable.) Let 𝓑 be a countable basis of Z, 𝓢 := {closure B : B ∈ 𝓑, closure B compact} (a countable family of compact sets) and 𝓣 a countable basis of ℝ. For K ∈ 𝓢 and a finite t ⊆ 𝓣 let [K, t] := {f ∈ C(Z, ℝ) : f(K) ⊆ ⋃t}; these sets form a countable family 𝓖 (𝓢 is countable and the finite subsets of the countable set 𝓣 form a countable set). Claim: the compact-open topology equals the topology generated by 𝓖. (i) Each [K, t] is compact-open open, since K is compact and ⋃t is open. (ii) Conversely let f ∈ C(Z, ℝ), K ⊆ Z compact, U ⊆ ℝ open with f(K) ⊆ U. Writing U as a union of members of 𝓣 and using compactness of f(K), choose a finite t ⊆ 𝓣 with f(K) ⊆ ⋃t and every V ∈ t contained in U. For x ∈ K pick V ∈ t with f(x) ∈ V; f⁻¹(V) is an open neighbourhood of x, so by local compactness it contains a compact neighbourhood C of x, and there is B ∈ 𝓑 with x ∈ B ⊆ C; then L_x := closure B ⊆ C is compact, so L_x ∈ 𝓢, L_x is a neighbourhood of x, and f(L_x) ⊆ V ⊆ ⋃t. Finitely many L_{x_1}, …, L_{x_p} cover K (compactness of K), f ∈ ⋂_j [L_{x_j}, t], and every g in this finite intersection satisfies g(K) ⊆ ⋃_j g(L_{x_j}) ⊆ ⋃t ⊆ U. Hence every compact-open neighbourhood of f contains a 𝓖-neighbourhood of f, and the two topologies coincide; so C(Z, ℝ) is second countable. (This is Mathlib's ContinuousMap.compactOpen_eq_generateFrom / ContinuousMap.instSecondCountableTopology from Mathlib.Topology.ContinuousMap.SecondCountableSpace, which the frozen contract does not import; it must be inlined, as in the accepted lemma cited above.)

2. (Dense sequence; uniform approximation on compacts.) By 1, C(Z, ℝ) is separable; let d : ℕ → C(Z, ℝ) have dense range (TopologicalSpace.denseSeq). The compact-open topology of C(Z, ℝ) is the topology of the uniformity of uniform convergence on compact sets (ContinuousMap.hasBasis_compactConvergenceUniformity). Hence for every h ∈ C(Z, ℝ), compact L ⊆ Z and η > 0, {g : |h(z) − g(z)| < η for all z ∈ L} is a neighbourhood of h and so contains some d_i: there is i with |h(z) − d_i(z)| < η for all z ∈ L.

3. (Exhaustion of the ball and cut-offs.) For m ∈ ℕ let r_m := R·(m+1)/(m+2). Then 0 < r_m < R, r_m < r_{m+1}, and r_m → R. Let E_m := closedBall(0, r_m) ×ˢ univ ⊆ Z (compact). E_m and the closed set (ball(0, r_{m+1}) ×ˢ univ)ᶜ are disjoint (closedBall(0, r_m) ⊆ ball(0, r_{m+1})), so Urysohn's lemma for locally compact Hausdorff spaces (exists_continuous_one_zero_of_isCompact) gives χ_m ∈ C(Z, ℝ) with χ_m = 1 on E_m, χ_m = 0 off ball(0, r_{m+1}) × G, χ_m(z) ∈ [0, 1] for all z, and χ_m with compact support. Since support χ_m ⊆ ball(0, r_{m+1}) × G, tsupport χ_m ⊆ closedBall(0, r_{m+1}) × G ⊆ B°_R(0) × G (r_{m+1} < R).

4. (The family.) For k ∈ ℕ let (m, i) := Nat.unpair k and g_k := χ_m · d_i. It is continuous with compact support (HasCompactSupport.mul_right), so g_k ∈ C_c(Z, ℝ), and tsupport g_k ⊆ tsupport χ_m ⊆ B°_R(0) × G (tsupport_mul_subset_left). This is the first assertion.

5. (Approximation.) Let f ∈ C_c(Z, ℝ) with tsupport f ⊆ B°_R(0) × G and η > 0.
(a) There is m with tsupport f ⊆ E_m: the open sets ball(0, r_m) × G increase with m and cover B°_R(0) × G (if |y| < R, then |y| < r_m for large m since r_m → R), hence cover the compact set tsupport f, so a single one of them contains it (IsCompact.elim_directed_cover for a monotone family); and ball(0, r_m) × G ⊆ E_m.
(b) By step 2 with h := f and L := E_{m+1}, choose i with |f(z) − d_i(z)| < η for all z ∈ E_{m+1}, and put k := Nat.pair m i, so g_k = χ_m · d_i.
(c) f = χ_m · f on Z: on E_m, χ_m = 1; off E_m, z ∉ tsupport f, so f(z) = 0. Hence f − g_k = χ_m · (f − d_i) and |f(z) − g_k(z)| = χ_m(z)·|f(z) − d_i(z)|. If z ∈ E_{m+1} this is ≤ 1·η. If z ∉ E_{m+1}, then z ∉ ball(0, r_{m+1}) × G, so χ_m(z) = 0 and the difference is 0 ≤ η. Thus |f(z) − g_k(z)| ≤ η for every z. ∎

## Key steps

1. C(ℝ^N × G, ℝ) with the compact-open topology is second countable (countable subbasis [K, t]; inlined since Mathlib.Topology.ContinuousMap.SecondCountableSpace is not imported).
2. A dense sequence d_i approximates every continuous function uniformly on any compact set (compact-convergence uniformity).
3. Exhaust B°_R(0) × G by compacts E_m = B_{r_m}(0) × G, r_m = R(m+1)/(m+2), with Urysohn cut-offs χ_m = 1 on E_m and tsupport χ_m ⊆ E_{m+1} ⊆ B°_R(0) × G.
4. g_{pair(m,i)} := χ_m · d_i are C_c with tsupport in B°_R(0) × G.
5. For admissible f: tsupport f ⊆ E_m for some m, f = χ_m f, and |f − χ_m d_i| = χ_m|f − d_i| ≤ η when d_i is η-close to f on E_{m+1}.
