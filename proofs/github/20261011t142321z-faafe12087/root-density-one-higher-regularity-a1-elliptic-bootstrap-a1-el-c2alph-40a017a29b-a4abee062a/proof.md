## Proof

Source: Step 7 of the parent proof (local to global), including the conversion between iteratedFDeriv of order 1 and fderiv.
1. C^1 on U. Every x in U has the open neighbourhood B_r(x) on which f is C^1, so f is C^1 on U intersected with B_r(x). By the local characterization of ContDiffOn (contDiffOn_of_locally_contDiffOn), f is C^1 on U.
2. Norm identity. For all x, y: ||iteratedFDeriv R 1 f x - iteratedFDeriv R 1 f y|| = ||Df(x) - Df(y)||. Indeed continuousMultilinearCurryFin1 is a linear isometric equivalence from (R^n [x1]->L R^l) to L(R^n, R^l) sending iteratedFDeriv R 1 f z to Df(z) for every z (iteratedFDeriv_one_apply), so it maps the difference to the difference and preserves norms. It therefore suffices to bound ||Df(x) - Df(y)|| on compact sets.
3. Fix a compact K contained in U. Since U is open and f is C^1 on U, Df is continuous on U (ContDiffOn.continuousOn_fderiv_of_isOpen), hence bounded on K: there is B with ||Df(y)|| <= B for y in K; put B' := max(B, 0).
4. Finite subcover. For each x in K choose r_x > 0 and C_x as in the hypothesis. The open balls B_{r_x}(x), x in K, cover K; by compactness finitely many of them, B_{r_i}(x_i) for i in a finite set T, cover K (IsCompact.elim_finite_subcover). Put C* := sum over i in T of |C_i|; then C* >= 0 and C* >= C_i for all i in T.
5. Lebesgue number. By lebesgue_number_lemma_of_metric applied to the finite open cover of the compact K, there is delta > 0 such that for every y in K there is i in T with B_delta(y) contained in B_{r_i}(x_i).
6. Let C' := C* + 2B'/delta^alpha. Let y, z in K.
 (a) If ||y - z|| < delta: choose i with B_delta(y) contained in B_{r_i}(x_i). Then y and z lie in B_{r_i}(x_i), so ||Df(y) - Df(z)|| <= C_i ||y - z||^alpha <= C* ||y - z||^alpha <= C' ||y - z||^alpha (using ||y - z||^alpha >= 0 and 2B'/delta^alpha >= 0).
 (b) If ||y - z|| >= delta: then delta^alpha <= ||y - z||^alpha (alpha > 0), and ||Df(y) - Df(z)|| <= ||Df(y)|| + ||Df(z)|| <= 2B' = (2B'/delta^alpha) delta^alpha <= (2B'/delta^alpha) ||y - z||^alpha <= C' ||y - z||^alpha.
7. By steps 2 and 6, ||iteratedFDeriv R 1 f y - iteratedFDeriv R 1 f z|| <= C' ||y - z||^alpha for y, z in K; with step 1 this is IsLocallyHolderCkα 1 alpha f U. QED
