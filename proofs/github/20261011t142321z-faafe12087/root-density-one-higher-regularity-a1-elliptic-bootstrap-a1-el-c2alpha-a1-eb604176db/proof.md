Source: Steps 3-4 of the parent proof (difference quotients of the system (E) as in [GM] Proposition 8.6, then the Schauder estimate of [GM] Theorem 5.19), in the classical form: the Schauder estimate (sibling dohr_eb_schauder_interior_estimate) is applied to the difference quotients themselves, uniformly in d, and the limit d -> 0 is taken with the sibling dohr_eb_difference_quotient_limit. This replaces the W^{2,2} detour of the parent Step 3 and needs no Sobolev spaces.
Notation: L = L(R^n, R^l), A(P) := DPsi(P) (a linear form on L), B(P) := D^2 Psi(P) (bilinear form, B(P)[Q][Q'] = fderiv R (fderiv R Psi) P Q Q'), e_k = EuclideanSpace.single k 1.
0. Preliminaries. IsLocallyHolderCkα 1 alpha u U says: u is C^1 on U and Du is alpha-Hoelder on each compact subset of U, since ||iteratedFDeriv R 1 u x - iteratedFDeriv R 1 u y|| = ||Du(x) - Du(y)|| (linear isometry, iteratedFDeriv_one_apply / norm_iteratedFDeriv_one). IsLocallyHolderCkα 0 alpha g U says: g is continuous on U and alpha-Hoelder on each compact subset (||iteratedFDeriv R 0 g x - iteratedFDeriv R 0 g y|| = |g(x) - g(y)|). If U is empty the conclusion is trivial; otherwise eps >= 0.
1. Constants of Psi on Kc := closedBall(0, eps) in L (compact, convex). Psi is C^3, so the functions P -> B(P)[Q][Q'] are C^1. Let M_B := sup{ |B(P)[Q][Q']| : P in Kc, ||Q||, ||Q'|| <= 1 } < infinity, and let L_B be a constant with |B(P)[Q][Q'] - B(P')[Q][Q']| <= L_B ||P - P'|| ||Q|| ||Q'|| for P, P' in Kc (mean value inequality on the convex Kc for the C^1 functions P -> B(P)[E_r][E_s] on a basis E_r of L, plus bilinearity; this avoids norms on the space of bilinear forms).
2. Localization. Fix xb in U and R > 0 with closedBall(xb, 4R) contained in U; K0 := closedBall(xb, 4R) is compact. Let H1 be a Hoelder constant of Du on K0 and H2 one of g on K0. Fix k in {1,...,n}, e := e_k (|e| = 1), and for 0 < |d| < R put w_d(x) := d^{-1}(u(x + de) - u(x)).
3. For x in Bb := B_{2R}(xb), both x and x + de lie in B_{3R}(xb), contained in U. Hence w_d is C^1 on Bb, Dw_d(x) = d^{-1}(Du(x + de) - Du(x)), ||Dw_d(x) - Dw_d(y)|| <= 2 H1 |d|^{-1} |x - y|^alpha on Bb (qualitative Hoelder bound), and |w_d(x)| = |integral_0^1 Du(x + t d e) e dt| <= eps.
4. Linear system for w_d. For x in Bb let P_t(x) := Du(x) + t(Du(x + de) - Du(x)) (t in [0, 1]); P_t(x) lies in Kc as a convex combination of two points of Kc. Define the bilinear form a_d(x)[P][Q] := integral_0^1 B(P_t(x))[P][Q] dt (bilinear and continuous; as a Lean object it can be built with LinearMap.toContinuousLinearMap from the L*-valued integral P -> integral_0^1 B(P_t(x))[P] dt), G_d(x) := integral_0^1 g(x + t d e) dt in R^l, and the linear form Phi_d(x)[P] := <G_d(x), P e>. Claim: for every zeta in C^1_c(R^n; R^l) with tsupport in Bb, integral over Bb of a_d(x)[Dw_d(x)][Dzeta(x)] = integral over Bb of Phi_d(x)[Dzeta(x)].
   (a) Test the hypothesis with phi1 := zeta(. - de) and phi2 := zeta (both C^1 with compact support and tsupport in B_{3R}(xb), contained in U). The integrands vanish outside tsupport phi (there phi = 0 and Dphi = 0), so all integrals may be taken over R^n. Translating x = y + de in the phi1-equation (translation invariance of Lebesgue measure): integral A(Du(y + de))[Dzeta(y)] dy = - integral <zeta(y), g(y + de)> dy. Subtracting the phi2-equation: integral [A(Du(y + de)) - A(Du(y))][Dzeta(y)] dy = - integral <zeta(y), g(y + de) - g(y)> dy.
   (b) For each y, A(Du(y + de)) - A(Du(y)) = integral_0^1 B(P_t(y))[Du(y + de) - Du(y)] dt (fundamental theorem of calculus for the C^1 map t -> A(P_t(y)), whose derivative is B(P_t(y))[Du(y + de) - Du(y)]) = d a_d(y)[Dw_d(y)]. So the left side equals d times integral a_d[Dw_d][Dzeta].
   (c) The right side equals, by translation, integral <zeta(x) - zeta(x - de), g(x)> dx, and zeta(x) - zeta(x - de) = d integral_0^1 Dzeta(x - t d e) e dt; by Fubini (continuous integrand with compact support in [0,1] x R^n) and the translation x = y + t d e this is d integral <G_d(y), Dzeta(y) e> dy = d integral Phi_d[Dzeta].
   (d) Divide by d != 0. (All integrands used in (a)-(c) are continuous on R^n with compact support even though g, Du need not be controlled outside U: each integrand vanishes on a neighbourhood of every point outside the relevant translate of tsupport zeta, which is a compact subset of U on which g and Du are continuous; this justifies the translations and Fubini.)
5. Uniform hypotheses of the sibling dohr_eb_schauder_interior_estimate on Bb = B_{2R}(xb), with constants independent of d and k: (H1) a_d(x)[P][P] = integral_0^1 B(P_t(x))[P][P] dt >= lam ||P||^2 (P_t(x) in Kc and the Legendre hypothesis); (H2) |a_d(x)[P][Q]| <= M_B ||P|| ||Q||; (H3) |a_d(x)[P][Q] - a_d(y)[P][Q]| <= L_B sup_t ||P_t(x) - P_t(y)|| ||P|| ||Q|| <= L_B H1 |x - y|^alpha ||P|| ||Q||, since ||P_t(x) - P_t(y)|| <= (1 - t)||Du(x) - Du(y)|| + t||Du(x + de) - Du(y + de)|| and all four points lie in K0; (H4) |Phi_d(x)[P] - Phi_d(y)[P]| <= |G_d(x) - G_d(y)| ||P|| <= H2 |x - y|^alpha ||P||; (H5) |w_d| <= eps; (H6), (H7) by step 3; (H8) by step 4. With M := max(M_B, L_B H1, H2, eps, 0), the sibling applied to (n, l, alpha, lam, M, 2R) gives C, independent of d and k, with ||Dw_d(x)|| <= C and ||Dw_d(x) - Dw_d(y)|| <= C |x - y|^alpha for x, y in B_R(xb) and all 0 < |d| < R.
6. Apply the sibling dohr_eb_difference_quotient_limit with f := u, x0 := xb, e := e_k, rho := R, delta := R, M := C: u is C^1 on B_{R + R|e|}(xb) = B_{2R}(xb), contained in U, and its two hypotheses are the bounds of step 5 because d^{-1}(Du(x + de) - Du(x)) = Dw_d(x). Hence v_k := (x -> Du(x) e_k) is C^1 on B_R(xb) with ||Dv_k|| <= C and ||Dv_k(x) - Dv_k(y)|| <= C |x - y|^alpha there.
7. Local to global. (a) Every point of U is the centre xb of such a ball, so v_k is C^1 on U (contDiffOn_of_locally_contDiffOn). (b) Let K be a compact subset of U. Cover K by the balls B_{R(xb)}(xb), xb in K, chosen as in step 2, take a finite subcover B_{R_i}(xb_i), i <= N, with constants C_i from step 6, and let C* := max_i C_i. By the Lebesgue number lemma (lebesgue_number_lemma_of_metric) there is eta > 0 such that for every x in K the ball B_eta(x) lies in some B_{R_i}(xb_i). For x, y in K: if |x - y| < eta, both lie in one B_{R_i}(xb_i) and ||Dv_k(x) - Dv_k(y)|| <= C* |x - y|^alpha; otherwise ||Dv_k(x) - Dv_k(y)|| <= 2C* <= 2C* eta^{-alpha} |x - y|^alpha. Since ||iteratedFDeriv R 1 v_k x - iteratedFDeriv R 1 v_k y|| = ||Dv_k(x) - Dv_k(y)||, IsLocallyHolderCkα 1 alpha v_k U holds for every k.
8. Conclusion. u is C^1 on U and every v_k = (x -> fderiv R u x e_k) satisfies IsLocallyHolderCkα 1 alpha v_k U; by the sibling dohr_eb_holder_succ_iff_fderiv (direction (C) => (A) with m = 1 and E = R^l, whose (A) is exactly IsLocallyHolderCkα 2 alpha u U), IsLocallyHolderCkα 2 alpha u U. QED

## Key steps

1. constants M_B, L_B for D^2 Psi on the convex compact eps-ball
2. difference quotients w_d solve a linear divergence system with averaged coefficients a_d and right side Phi_d
3. a_d is Legendre (segment stays in the eps-ball), bounded and alpha-Hoelder; Phi_d alpha-Hoelder; all uniformly in d
4. Schauder estimate (sibling) gives uniform C^{1,alpha} bounds on w_d on B_R(xb)
5. difference-quotient limit (sibling): D_k u is C^1 with alpha-Hoelder derivative on B_R(xb)
6. Lebesgue-number covering gives D_k u in C^{1,alpha}_loc(U); holder_succ_iff_fderiv gives u in C^{2,alpha}_loc(U)
