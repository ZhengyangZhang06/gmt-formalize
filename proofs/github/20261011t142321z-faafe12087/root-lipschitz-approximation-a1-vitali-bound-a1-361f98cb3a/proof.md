## Proof

Source: Simon Ch. 1, 3.4 (5-times covering lemma) followed by the summation used in the proof of Lemma 2.6 (Ch. 5) and in steps 3 and 9 of the parent proof; fact (Cov) of the parent proof.

1. If S is empty, nu(S) = 0 and there is nothing to prove. Otherwise consider the family of closed balls B_y := B_(r(y))(y), y in S; by hypothesis 0 < r(y) <= R_0, so the radii are bounded.
2. 5-times covering lemma (Simon Ch. 1, 3.4; Mathlib Vitali.exists_disjoint_subfamily_covering_enlargement_closedBall with tau = 5 > 3): there is a subset S' of S such that the balls B_b, b in S', are pairwise disjoint and for every a in S there is b in S' with B_a contained in B_(5 r(b))(b). Since r(a) > 0, a lies in B_a, hence a lies in S cap B_(5 r(b))(b).
3. S' is countable: the open balls B^o_(r(b))(b), b in S', are nonempty (r(b) > 0), open and pairwise disjoint (being contained in the disjoint closed balls), and R^N is separable (Mathlib Set.PairwiseDisjoint.countable_of_isOpen).
4. By step 2, S is contained in the countable union over b in S' of S cap B_(5 r(b))(b). By countable subadditivity of nu and the hypothesis, nu(S) <= sum_(b in S') nu(S cap B_(5 r(b))(b)) <= sum_(b in S') M Psi(B_b) = M sum_(b in S') Psi(B_b) (ENNReal.tsum_mul_left; valid also for M = infinity).
5. The closed balls B_b, b in S', are measurable, pairwise disjoint and contained in W, and S' is countable, so sum_(b in S') Psi(B_b) = Psi(union_(b in S') B_b) <= Psi(W). Hence nu(S) <= M Psi(W).

## Key steps

1. Bounded radii 0 < r(y) <= R_0
2. Vitali 5r selection: disjoint subfamily S' with every B_a inside some B_(5 r(b))(b)
3. S' is countable (disjoint nonempty open interiors in a separable space)
4. Countable subadditivity of nu and the hypothesis bound nu(S) by M times the sum of Psi(B_b)
5. Countable additivity of Psi on the disjoint balls inside W gives nu(S) <= M Psi(W)
