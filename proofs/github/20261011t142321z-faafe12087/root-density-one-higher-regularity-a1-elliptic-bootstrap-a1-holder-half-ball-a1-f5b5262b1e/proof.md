## Proof

Source: Step 6 of the parent proof ('each D^i u (i <= q+2) is continuous on the compact closed ball, hence bounded there; D^{q+2} u is alpha-Hoelder on the compact closed ball, hence on the open ball').
1. If r <= 0 then ball x0 (r/2) is empty and the three conditions of IsHolderCkα hold vacuously (ContDiffOn on the empty set; take B = 0 and K = 0). Assume r > 0.
2. ContDiffOn: ball x0 (r/2) is contained in ball x0 r, so ContDiffOn R j u (ball x0 (r/2)) follows from the first component of the hypothesis by monotonicity.
3. Bounds: K := closedBall x0 (r/2) is compact (proper space) and contained in ball x0 r since r/2 < r. As ball x0 r is open and u is C^j on it, for each i <= j the map iteratedFDeriv R i u is continuous on ball x0 r (ContDiffOn.continuousOn_iteratedFDerivWithin together with iteratedFDerivWithin_of_isOpen), hence bounded on K by some B_i. Put B := max_{i <= j} B_i; then ||iteratedFDeriv R i u x|| <= B for all i <= j and x in ball x0 (r/2), which is contained in K.
4. Hoelder: the second component of IsLocallyHolderCkα j alpha u (ball x0 r), applied to the compact K contained in ball x0 r, gives C with ||D^j u(x) - D^j u(y)|| <= C ||x - y||^alpha for x, y in K, in particular for x, y in ball x0 (r/2). Take this C as the constant in the third component of IsHolderCkα. QED
