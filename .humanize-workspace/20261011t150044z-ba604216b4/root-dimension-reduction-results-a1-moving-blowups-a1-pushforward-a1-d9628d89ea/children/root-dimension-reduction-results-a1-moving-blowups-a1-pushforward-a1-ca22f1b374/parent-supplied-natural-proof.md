## Key steps

1. B_i⁺ := {a ∈ B_i : J_i(a) > 0, w_i(a) ≥ 1} is Borel and DH_i is injective on it.
2. Sibling bilip_partition cuts B_i⁺ into countably many disjoint Borel pieces C_{il} on which H_i is bi-Lipschitz.
3. Sibling piece_imrep turns each piece into an IMRep R_{il} with mass ∫_{C_{il}} w_iJ_i and action ∫_{C_{il}} w_iζ_i(ω∘H_i).
4. The masses are summable, so sibling imrep_tsum gives one IMRep R with Σ_{(i,l)} R_{il}.action(ω) = R.action(ω).
5. Sibling jac_algebra (b) dominates the integrands by w_iJ_i‖ω‖_∞ and makes them vanish off B_i⁺; countable additivity and fiberwise summation give Σ_i ∫_{B_i} w_iζ_i(ω∘H_i) = R.action(ω).
