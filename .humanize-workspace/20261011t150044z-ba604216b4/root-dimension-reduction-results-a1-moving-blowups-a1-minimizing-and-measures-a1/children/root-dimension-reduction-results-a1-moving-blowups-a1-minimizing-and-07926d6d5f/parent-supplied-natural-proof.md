## Proof

Source: Simon 7.2.4 (8)–(12) and the lower-semicontinuity step after (12) (chapter-07.txt lines 222–263); step 1 of the parent proof. Siblings used: mass_lsc (M_O(C) ≤ liminf_i M_O(S_{φ(i)}) for every set O and strictly increasing φ, under (1), (2)) and minimality_localizes (accepted cmc_massOn_le_of_le_near_support: if U ⊆ ℝ^P is open, S, T ∈ 𝒟_n(U) are integer multiplicity, K is compact with spt(S − T) ⊆ K, W ⊆ W' are open with K ⊆ W, closure W' compact and contained in U, and M_W(T) ≤ M_W(S), then M_{W'}(T) ≤ M_{W'}(S)). The latter packages exactly the locality argument (L4) and the additivity computation M_W(C) = μ_C(W') + μ_C(W ∖ W') ≤ … of parent step 1.
1. The first component of IsMinimizingIn C ℝ^P, that C is integer multiplicity, is a hypothesis.
2. Let W ⊆ ℝ^P be open with closure W compact (and closure W ⊆ ℝ^P), let S ∈ 𝒟_n(ℝ^P) be integer multiplicity with SameBoundary S C, and suppose K := spt(S − C) is compact with K ⊆ ℝ^P ∩ W, so K ⊆ W. We must show M_W(C) ≤ M_W(S).
3. Since K is compact, W is open and K ⊆ W, there is ε > 0 with thickening(ε, K) ⊆ W (a compact subset of an open set has a uniform metric neighbourhood inside it; Mathlib IsCompact.exists_thickening_subset_open).
4. Apply hypothesis (3) to K, ε and the strictly increasing map ψ := id. It yields an open W' with K ⊆ W' ⊆ thickening(ε, K) and a strictly increasing φ' such that limsup_i M_{W'}(S_{φ'(i)}) ≤ M_{W'}(S') for every admissible competitor S'. The current S is admissible: it is integer multiplicity, SameBoundary S C holds, and spt(S − C) = K ⊆ K. Hence limsup_i M_{W'}(S_{φ'(i)}) ≤ M_{W'}(S).
5. The sibling mass_lsc, applied with φ := φ' and O := W' (its hypotheses are (1) and (2)), gives M_{W'}(C) ≤ liminf_i M_{W'}(S_{φ'(i)}). For every sequence in [0, ∞], liminf ≤ limsup along atTop. Therefore M_{W'}(C) ≤ liminf_i M_{W'}(S_{φ'(i)}) ≤ limsup_i M_{W'}(S_{φ'(i)}) ≤ M_{W'}(S).
6. Apply the sibling minimality_localizes (cmc_massOn_le_of_le_near_support, whose frozen statement names the smaller open set W and the larger one W') with the instantiation (U, S, T, K, W, W') := (ℝ^P, S, C, K, W', W), where W' is the open set from step 4 and W the given open set: ℝ^P is open; S and C are integer multiplicity; K is compact and spt(S − C) ⊆ K; W' and W are open; K ⊆ W' by step 4; W' ⊆ thickening(ε, K) ⊆ W by steps 3–4; closure W is compact and contained in ℝ^P (CompactlyContained W ℝ^P); and M_{W'}(C) ≤ M_{W'}(S) by step 5. Its conclusion is M_W(C) ≤ M_W(S).
7. Since W and S were arbitrary, C is minimizing in ℝ^P. ∎

## Key steps

1. Given an admissible pair (W, S), K := spt(S − C) ⊆ W is compact; choose ε > 0 with thickening(ε, K) ⊆ W.
2. Hypothesis (3) with ψ = id and the competitor S gives an open W' ⊇ K inside thickening(ε, K) and φ' with limsup_i M_{W'}(S_{φ'(i)}) ≤ M_{W'}(S); mass_lsc and liminf ≤ limsup give M_{W'}(C) ≤ M_{W'}(S).
3. minimality_localizes transfers M_{W'}(C) ≤ M_{W'}(S) from W' to W.
