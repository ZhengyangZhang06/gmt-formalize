## Key steps

1. If n > P every n-form on ℝ^P vanishes, so all currents are 0 and 𝒬 = {0} works.
2. Pick ρ with 2cρΛ < ε; the integer polyhedral chains on the finitely many grid cells inside cthickening(r(ρ), K) with coefficients ≤ cΛ/ρⁿ form a finite set 𝒬.
3. Extend A and ∂A by zero to IM currents Ǎ, Ž on ℝ^P (C1 shows the carriers lie a.e. in K; (IMC)(b) gives the currents) and check ∂Ǎ = Ž with a cutoff.
4. Apply the deformation theorem (n < P) or its top-dimensional case (n = P) to Ǎ: Ǎ − Π = ∂R̂ + Ŝ with Π a chain in 𝒬 and small M(R̂), M(Ŝ).
5. Restrict R̂, Ŝ, Π to U: restrictions stay IM, masses do not increase, and A − Q = ∂R + S with M(R) + M(S) ≤ 2cρΛ < ε.
