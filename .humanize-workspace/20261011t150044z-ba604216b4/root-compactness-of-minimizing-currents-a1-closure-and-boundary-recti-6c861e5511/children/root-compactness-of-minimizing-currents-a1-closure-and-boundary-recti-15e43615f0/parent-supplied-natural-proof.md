## Proof

Source: step 4 of the parent proof ("growth forces r ≤ n"), with ω_n replaced by an arbitrary finite constant C; there C = ω_n (hypothesis (2)) and ν(B_1(0)) > 0 is hypothesis (3) at r = 1.
1. Let r := dim V and m₀ := ν(B_1(0)). Then 0 < m₀ ≤ C·1 < ∞ (growth with R = 1). If r = 0 there is nothing to prove; assume r ≥ 1 and let e'_1, …, e'_r be an orthonormal basis of V.
2. (Equal mass of translated unit balls.) For u ∈ V we have −u ∈ V and {x : −u + x ∈ B_1(0)} = B_1(u), so ν(B_1(u)) = ν(B_1(0)) = m₀ by the invariance hypothesis (open balls are Borel).
3. (Packing.) Fix an integer M ≥ 1 and let Λ_M := {k ∈ ℤ^r : |k_j| ≤ M for all j}, a set with (2M+1)^r elements. For k ∈ Λ_M put u_k := 2Σ_j k_je'_j ∈ V. By orthonormality ‖u_k − u_l‖ = 2‖k − l‖_{ℓ²} ≥ 2 for k ≠ l, so the balls B_1(u_k), k ∈ Λ_M, are pairwise disjoint; and ‖u_k‖ = 2‖k‖_{ℓ²} ≤ 2M√r, so B_1(u_k) ⊆ B_{R_M}(0) with R_M := 2M√r + 1 ≤ 3M√r (as M√r ≥ 1).
4. (Counting.) By steps 2–3, additivity and monotonicity, and the growth hypothesis at R_M > 0: (2M+1)^r·m₀ = Σ_{k∈Λ_M}ν(B_1(u_k)) = ν(⋃_kB_1(u_k)) ≤ ν(B_{R_M}(0)) ≤ C·R_M^n ≤ C·(3√r)^n·M^n.
5. (Conclusion.) Suppose r > n. Since M^r ≤ (2M+1)^r, step 4 gives M^{r−n} ≤ C(3√r)^n/m₀ for every integer M ≥ 1. The right side is a finite real constant (C < ∞, m₀ > 0), while M^{r−n} → ∞ as M → ∞ — a contradiction. Hence r ≤ n. ∎

## Key steps

1. Translation invariance gives ν(B_1(u)) = ν(B_1(0)) = m₀ ∈ (0, ∞) for every u ∈ V.
2. The (2M+1)^r points 2Σk_je'_j (|k_j| ≤ M) of V have pairwise disjoint unit balls inside B_{3M√r}(0).
3. (2M+1)^r m₀ ≤ C(3M√r)^n for all M forces r ≤ n.
