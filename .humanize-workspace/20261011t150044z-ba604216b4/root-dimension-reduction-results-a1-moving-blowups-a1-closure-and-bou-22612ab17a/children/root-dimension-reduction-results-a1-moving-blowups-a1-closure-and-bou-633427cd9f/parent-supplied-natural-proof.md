## Key steps

1. Plane uniqueness: β(v) = β(v') for all n-covectors forces span v = span v' (det = 1 and a volume argument make p_{Q'}|_Q an isometry).
2. For any blow-up sequence, (BL) gives a limit C = θ₀ζ(a)ν with ISP; LD gives the lower density of ν.
3. (CP) makes ν a finite sum of parallel planes with ζ(a) = v∧; (WG) gives every weight ≥ 1, so ν = Hⁿ⌞span v; (PI) gives θ(a) ∈ ℕ.
4. Plane uniqueness and the subsequence criterion give the approximate tangent plane.
