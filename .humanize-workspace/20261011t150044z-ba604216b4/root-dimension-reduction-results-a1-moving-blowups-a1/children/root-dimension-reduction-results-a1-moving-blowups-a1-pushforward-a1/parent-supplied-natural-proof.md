## Key steps

1. Admissible Y: finite mass; Ȳ extends Y to forms continuous near spt Y, R-independent, additive.
2. push Y(ω) := Ȳ(f^#ω): formula, mass ≤ Lⁿ·M(Y) by (L1)–(L2), support in f(spt Y), linear.
3. Identity: secant argument gives Df(x) = id on T_xM.
4. Boundary: pushes by mollified g_σ are cycles (locality of d, VO) converging to push Y.
5. Integrality: bi-Lipschitz parametrization, tangent planes on pieces, area formula for f∘G_i give an IMRep.
