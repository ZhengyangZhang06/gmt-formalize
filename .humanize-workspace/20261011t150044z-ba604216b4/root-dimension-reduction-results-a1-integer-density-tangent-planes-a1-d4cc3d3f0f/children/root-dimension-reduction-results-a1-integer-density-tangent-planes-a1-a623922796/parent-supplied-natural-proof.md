3. (Compactness of the parameters.) S := closedBall(0, 1) × [R⁻¹, 1] ⊆ ℝ^P × ℝ is compact (a product of compact sets; closed balls of ℝ^P are compact). Every (x_i, λ_i) lies in S: ‖x_i‖ ≤ 1 − λ_i ≤ 1 and R⁻¹ ≤ λ_i ≤ 1 (step 2 and admissibility). By sequential compactness (IsCompact.tendsto_subseq) there are (x*, λ*) ∈ S and a strictly increasing ψ : ℕ → ℕ with (x_{ψ(i)}, λ_{ψ(i)}) → (x*, λ*); applying the continuous projections, x_{ψ(i)} → x* and λ_{ψ(i)} → λ*.
4. (Admissibility of the limit.) (x*, λ*) ∈ S gives λ* ≥ R⁻¹ > 0 and λ* ≤ 1. Both sides of ‖x_{ψ(i)}‖ ≤ 1 − λ_{ψ(i)} converge (continuity of the norm), so in the limit ‖x*‖ ≤ 1 − λ* (le_of_tendsto_of_tendsto'). Hence (x*, λ*) is admissible.
5. (Equal integrals.) Let f be continuous with compact support. By PC (μ₀ is finite, λ_{ψ(i)} > 0, λ* > 0, x_{ψ(i)} → x*, λ_{ψ(i)} → λ*), ∫ f dμ₀^{x_{ψ(i)},λ_{ψ(i)}} → ∫ f dμ₀^{x*,λ*}. By hypothesis composed with ψ (ψ(i) → ∞ since ψ is strictly increasing; StrictMono.tendsto_atTop), the same sequence converges to ∫ f dν. Limits in ℝ are unique (tendsto_nhds_unique), so ∫ f dν = ∫ f dμ₀^{x*,λ*}.
6. (ν = μ₀^{x*,λ*}.) ν is finite on compact sets by hypothesis. So is μ₀^{x*,λ*}: for every K', μ₀^{x*,λ*}(K') ≤ μ₀^{x*,λ*}(ℝ^P) = ofReal(λ*⁻¹^n)·μ₀((dilation x* λ*)⁻¹(ℝ^P)) = ofReal(λ*⁻¹^n)·μ₀(ℝ^P) < ∞ (monotonicity; Measure.smul_apply and Measure.map_apply, the dilation being continuous hence measurable). By step 5 and VU (with ν₁ := ν and ν₂ := μ₀^{x*,λ*}), ν = μ₀^{x*,λ*}.
7. (K ⊄ G_{x*,λ*}.) Suppose, for contradiction, that K ⊆ G_{x*,λ*}. K is nonempty: if K = ∅, then K ⊆ G_{x_0,λ_0}, contradicting the hypothesis at i = 0. The function h(v) := ‖x* + λ*•v‖ is continuous, so it attains its maximum M = h(v₀) on the nonempty compact set K (IsCompact.exists_isMaxOn). As v₀ ∈ K ⊆ G_{x*,λ*}, DG (a) (λ* > 0) gives M < 2. Put γ := 2 − M > 0. By step 3 there is an index i with ‖x_{ψ(i)} − x*‖ < γ/2 and |λ_{ψ(i)} − λ*| < γ/(2R) (each holds for all large i; Metric.tendsto_nhds). By DG (a) (λ_{ψ(i)} > 0), v ∈ G_{x_{ψ(i)},λ_{ψ(i)}}. Hence K ⊆ G_{x_{ψ(i)},λ_{ψ(i)}}, contradicting the hypothesis at the index ψ(i). Therefore K ⊄ G_{x*,λ*}.
8. (Conclusion.) (x, λ) := (x*, λ*) is admissible (step 4), ν = μ₀^{x,λ} (step 6) and K ⊄ G_{x,λ} (step 7). ∎

2. The admissible parameters with λ ≥ 1/R lie in the compact set closedBall(0,1) × [1/R, 1]; extract a convergent subsequence with admissible limit (x*, λ*).
