1. (Null transfer, NT.) If Z ⊆ M and Hⁿ(Z) = 0 then H^m(Z ∩ f⁻¹{t}) = 0 for a.e. t. Proof: let Z' := toMeasurable Hⁿ Z, a Borel set with Hⁿ(Z') = 0, and apply `coarea` to g := 1_{Z'}: ∫_ℝ H^m(Z' ∩ M ∩ f⁻¹{t}) dt = ∫_M |G|1_{Z'} dHⁿ ≤ L·Hⁿ(Z') = 0 (the inner integral of 1_{Z'} over M ∩ f⁻¹{t} is H^m(Z' ∩ M ∩ f⁻¹{t}) by `Measure.restrict_apply`). Hence H^m(Z ∩ f⁻¹{t}) ≤ H^m(Z' ∩ M ∩ f⁻¹{t}) = 0 for a.e. t (Z ⊆ M).
2. (Exhaustion and Borel modifications.) Choose compacts K_i ⊆ U (i ≥ 1) such that every compact K ⊆ U lies in some K_i (U open). Since M ⊆ U = ⋃K_i and θ is integrable on each M ∩ K_i, θ is Hⁿ⌞M-a.e. strongly measurable (`aestronglyMeasurable_iUnion_iff`); let θ̃, ξ̃ be Borel (strongly measurable) with θ = θ̃ and ξ = ξ̃ Hⁿ-a.e. on M. Let B ⊆ M be Borel with Hⁿ(M ∖ B) = 0. Z₁ := (M ∖ B) ∪ {x ∈ M : θ(x) ≠ θ̃(x) or ξ(x) ≠ ξ̃(x)} is Hⁿ-null.
3. (subset, pos.) For every t, M_t ⊆ M ⊆ U and θ > 0 on M_t.
4. (integrableOn.) Apply `coarea` to the Borel functions g_i := 1_{K_i}·ofReal(θ̃): ∫_ℝ ∫_{M∩f⁻¹{t}} g_i dH^m dt = ∫_M |G| g_i dHⁿ ≤ L∫_{M∩K_i} θ dHⁿ < ∞. For such t which also satisfy NT for Z₁ (so H^m(Z₁ ∩ f⁻¹{t}) = 0), θ = θ̃ H^m-a.e. on M_t ∩ K_i ⊆ M ∩ f⁻¹{t} ∩ K_i; as θ̃ is Borel, θ is H^m⌞(M_t ∩ K_i)-a.e. strongly measurable, and since θ > 0 on M_t, ∫_{M_t∩K_i} |θ| dH^m = ∫_{M_t∩K_i} ofReal(θ̃) dH^m ≤ ∫_{M∩f⁻¹{t}∩K_i} ofReal(θ̃) dH^m < ∞; so θ is H^m-integrable on M_t ∩ K_i. 5. (nullMeasurableSet.) M_t = (B ∩ f⁻¹{t} ∩ {G ≠ 0}) ∪ ((M ∖ B) ∩ f⁻¹{t} ∩ {G ≠ 0}); the first set is Borel (f continuous, G Borel) and the second is H^m-null for a.e. t by NT (Z₁ ⊇ M ∖ B). So M_t is H^m-null-measurable for a.e. t.
6. (aestronglyMeasurable.) The map x ↦ ι(x) := (alternatizeUncurryFinCLM).comp(smulRightL (innerSL ℝ (|G(x)|⁻¹G(x)))) ∈ L(Λ^m, Λ^{m+1}) is Borel (continuous linear operations applied to the Borel map x ↦ |G(x)|⁻¹G(x)), and composition of continuous linear maps is continuous; so ξ̃_t(x) := ξ̃(x).comp(ι(x)) is strongly measurable, and ξ_t = ξ̃_t on M_t ∖ Z₁. 7. (Charts and C¹ approximations.) Charts (Simon Ch. 3, Lemma 1.2 and Remark 1.3). Since M is countably n-rectifiable, M ⊆ N₀ ∪ ⋃_{j≥1} N_j with Hⁿ(N₀) = 0 and N_j embedded C¹ n-dimensional submanifolds of ℝ^P. Covering every N_j by countably many relatively compact coordinate patches we obtain countably many open Ω_j ⊆ ℝⁿ and C¹ maps Φ_j : ℝⁿ → ℝ^P which are Lipschitz on Ω_j and satisfy c_j|w − w'| ≤ |Φ_j(w) − Φ_j(w')| (w, w' ∈ Ω_j, some c_j > 0), with Hⁿ(M ∖ ⋃_j Φ_j(Ω_j)) = 0. Let Z₂ ⊆ M be the Hⁿ-null set consisting of M ∖ ⋃_{jk}Φ_j(A_{jk}) together with the points of M where the orientation clause of IsRectifiableTriple U n M θ ξ fails, where TG(f, G) fails, or where (for some j with x ∈ Φ_j(Ω_j)) the tangent identification with Ψ = Φ_j fails. ## Key steps

1. Null transfer from the sibling co-area formula: Hⁿ-null subsets of M have H^m-null slices for a.e. t.
2. Co-area with 1_{K_i}θ̃: θ is locally H^m-integrable on M_t for a.e. t; Borel modifications give measurability of M_t and ξ_t.
3. C¹ charts plus C¹ approximation: away from a null set, M_t lies in the C¹ m-manifolds Φ_j{h_{jk} = t, ∇h_{jk} ≠ 0} (implicit function theorem), so M_t is countably m-rectifiable (Simon Ch. 3, 2.10(2)).
4. Tangent plane of (M_t, θ) is T_xM ∩ G(x)^⊥ a.e. (Simon Ch. 3, 1.6, 1.8(1) in dimension m; direct counting-measure computation for m = 0).
5. Orientation (Simon 4.3): with an adapted orthonormal frame, ξ⌞(G/|G|) = ±simple frame of T_xM ∩ G^⊥.
