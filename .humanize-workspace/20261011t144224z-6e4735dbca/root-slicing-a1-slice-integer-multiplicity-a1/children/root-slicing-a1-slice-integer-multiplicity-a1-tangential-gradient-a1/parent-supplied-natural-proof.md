## Proof

Conventions. A k-covector is α ∈ Covector (Eucl P) k (continuous alternating k-form), ‖α‖ its operator norm; a k-vector is w ∈ NVector (Eucl P) k = (Covector (Eucl P) k →L[ℝ] ℝ); simple(τ) := simpleNVector τ, simple(τ)(α) = α(τ). For w ∈ ℝ^P and α ∈ Λ^k put w♭∧α := ContinuousAlternatingMap.alternatizeUncurryFin ((innerSL ℝ w).smulRight α) ∈ Λ^{k+1}; by `alternatizeUncurryFin_apply`, (w♭∧α)(v₀,…,v_k) = Σ_{i=0}^{k} (−1)^i ⟨w, v_i⟩ α(v₀,…,v̂_i,…,v_k); it is bilinear in (w, α), ‖w♭∧α‖ ≤ (k+1)|w|‖α‖, and it equals ((alternatizeUncurryFinCLM ℝ (Eucl P) ℝ).comp (smulRightL ℝ (Eucl P) (Covector (Eucl P) k) (innerSL ℝ w))) α by `rfl`. Fact U: if τ is orthonormal and w = ±simple(τ) then |w(α)| = |α(τ)| ≤ ‖α‖·∏|τ_i| = ‖α‖. Fact F (frame sign): if τ, τ' are orthonormal bases of the same k-dimensional subspace, τ'_i = Σ_j B_ij τ_j with B orthogonal, then by multilinearity and alternation simple(τ') = det(B)·simple(τ) and det B = ±1. IsRectifiableTriple U k M θ ξ means: M ⊆ U; M is Hᵏ-null-measurable; M is countably k-rectifiable; θ > 0 on M; θ is Hᵏ-integrable on M ∩ K for every compact K ⊆ U; ξ is Hᵏ⌞M-a.e. strongly measurable; for Hᵏ-a.e. x ∈ M there is an orthonormal τ : Fin k → ℝ^P with HasWeightedApproximateTangentSpaceAt k M θ x (span τ) and ξ(x) = ±simple(τ). The weighted approximate tangent plane (Simon Ch. 3, 1.7) is unique at points where θ(x) ≠ 0 (SimonGMT `HasWeightedApproximateTangentSpaceAt.unique`); when it exists at x ∈ M we call it T_x. TG(f, G) abbreviates the tangential-gradient property: for Hⁿ-a.e. x ∈ M and every Q with HasWeightedApproximateTangentSpaceAt n M θ x Q, G(x) ∈ Q and for every u ∈ Q the map s ↦ f(x + s u) has derivative ⟨G(x), u⟩ at s = 0.
1. Charts (Simon Ch. 3, Lemma 1.2 and Remark 1.3). Since M is countably n-rectifiable, M ⊆ N₀ ∪ ⋃_{j≥1} N_j with Hⁿ(N₀) = 0 and N_j embedded C¹ n-dimensional submanifolds of ℝ^P. Covering every N_j by countably many relatively compact coordinate patches we obtain countably many open Ω_j ⊆ ℝⁿ and C¹ maps Φ_j : ℝⁿ → ℝ^P which are Lipschitz on Ω_j and satisfy c_j|w − w'| ≤ |Φ_j(w) − Φ_j(w')| (w, w' ∈ Ω_j, some c_j > 0), with Hⁿ(M ∖ ⋃_j Φ_j(Ω_j)) = 0. Consequently Φ_j|Ω_j is injective, DΦ_j(w) is injective for w ∈ Ω_j (|DΦ_j(w)u| ≥ c_j|u|), Φ_j(Ω_j) is σ-compact hence Borel, (Φ_j|Ω_j)⁻¹ is (1/c_j)-Lipschitz hence Borel, Φ_j maps Lⁿ-null subsets of Ω_j to Hⁿ-null sets and Φ_j⁻¹ maps Hⁿ-null subsets of Φ_j(Ω_j) to Lⁿ-null sets. Put A_j(w) := DΦ_j(w) and T_j(w) := range A_j(w) (an n-dimensional subspace). Define pairwise disjoint Borel sets V_j := Φ_j(Ω_j) ∖ ⋃_{i<j} Φ_i(Ω_i); then Hⁿ(M ∖ ⋃_j V_j) = 0.
2. (Rademacher in charts.) f∘Φ_j is Lipschitz on Ω_j (constant L·Lip(Φ_j|Ω_j)); extending it to a Lipschitz function on ℝⁿ (McShane, `LipschitzOnWith.extend_real`) and applying Rademacher's theorem (`LipschitzWith.ae_differentiableAt`), using that Ω_j is open, f∘Φ_j is differentiable at Lⁿ-a.e. w ∈ Ω_j. Let D_j ⊆ Ω_j be the set of differentiability points; it is Borel (`measurableSet_of_differentiableAt`) and Lⁿ(Ω_j ∖ D_j) = 0, so Hⁿ(Φ_j(Ω_j ∖ D_j)) = 0.
3. (Definition of G.) For x ∈ V_j with w := (Φ_j|Ω_j)⁻¹(x) ∈ D_j put G(x) := A_j(w)(A_j(w)ᵀA_j(w))⁻¹∇(f∘Φ_j)(w), and put G(x) := 0 for all other x ∈ ℝ^P. Then G(x) ∈ T_j(w) and A_j(w)ᵀG(x) = ∇(f∘Φ_j)(w), i.e. ⟨G(x), A_j(w)u⟩ = D(f∘Φ_j)(w)u for all u ∈ ℝⁿ. G is Borel: Φ_j(D_j) is Borel as the image of a Borel set under the continuous injective map Φ_j|Ω_j (Lusin–Souslin, `MeasurableSet.image_of_continuousOn_injOn`), and on the Borel set V_j ∩ Φ_j(D_j) it is the composition of the Borel map (Φ_j|Ω_j)⁻¹ with the Borel map w ↦ A_j(w)(A_j(w)ᵀA_j(w))⁻¹∇(f∘Φ_j)(w) (DΦ_j is continuous, A_jᵀA_j is invertible on Ω_j, and w ↦ fderiv ℝ (f∘Φ_j) w is Borel by `measurable_fderiv`), and it is 0 on the complement of the countable union of these Borel sets.
4. Directional derivatives (Simon Ch. 2, 4.10). Let Φ be C¹ near w, x = Φ(w), F : ℝ^P → ℝ Lipschitz with constant C, and suppose F∘Φ is differentiable at w. Then for every u ∈ ℝⁿ, s ↦ F(x + s DΦ(w)u) has derivative D(F∘Φ)(w)u at s = 0: indeed F(x + sDΦ(w)u) − F(x) = [F(Φ(w+su)) − F(Φ(w))] + [F(x + sDΦ(w)u) − F(Φ(w+su))], the first bracket is sD(F∘Φ)(w)u + o(s), and the second is bounded by C|Φ(w) + sDΦ(w)u − Φ(w+su)| = o(s). Applied with F = f, Φ = Φ_j and w ∈ D_j it gives: for x = Φ_j(w) ∈ V_j and every v = A_j(w)u ∈ T_j(w), s ↦ f(x + sv) has derivative D(f∘Φ_j)(w)u = ⟨G(x), v⟩ at s = 0.
5. (Bound.) In the situation of Step 4, |f(x + sv) − f(x)| ≤ L|s||v|, so |⟨G(x), v⟩| ≤ L|v| for v ∈ T_j(w); taking v = G(x) ∈ T_j(w) gives |G(x)|² ≤ L|G(x)|, hence |G(x)| ≤ L. At all other points G = 0. So |G(x)| ≤ L for every x.
6. Tangent identification (Simon Ch. 3, Remark 1.5(2), proof of Theorem 1.6 '⟹' and Remark 1.8(1), weighted version): if M' ⊆ U is Hᵏ-null-measurable, θ > 0 on M' and θ is Hᵏ-integrable on M' ∩ K for compact K ⊆ U, and Ψ : ℝᵏ → ℝ^P is C¹ on an open V ⊆ ℝᵏ, Lipschitz on V with a positive lower Lipschitz bound on V, then for Hᵏ-a.e. x ∈ M' ∩ Ψ(V) the pair (M', θ) has weighted approximate tangent plane range DΨ(Ψ⁻¹x) at x. (For φ ∈ C_c(ℝ^P), r^{-k}∫_{M'} φ((z−x)/r)θ(z) dHᵏ(z) splits into θ(x)·r^{-k}∫_{Ψ(V)} φ((z−x)/r) dHᵏ, which tends to θ(x)∫_{range DΨ} φ dHᵏ because Ψ(V) is a C¹ k-submanifold at x; the error r^{-k}∫_{M'∩Ψ(V)} φ(·)(θ − θ(x)) dHᵏ, which tends to 0 at Lebesgue points of θ for the Radon measure Hᵏ⌞(M'∩Ψ(V)∩K); and the parts over Ψ(V) ∖ M' and M' ∖ Ψ(V), which tend to 0 at Hᵏ-a.e. point of M' ∩ Ψ(V) by the upper-density theorem, Simon Ch. 1, 3.6, since Hᵏ⌞(Ψ(V)∖M') and θHᵏ⌞(M'∖Ψ(V)) are locally finite and vanish on M' ∩ Ψ(V).) Uniqueness at θ(x) > 0 then identifies every weighted approximate tangent plane at such x with range DΨ(Ψ⁻¹x). Apply it with k = n, M' = M and Ψ = Φ_j, V = Ω_j: for Hⁿ-a.e. x ∈ M ∩ Φ_j(Ω_j), every Q with HasWeightedApproximateTangentSpaceAt n M θ x Q equals T_j((Φ_j|Ω_j)⁻¹x).
7. (Conclusion.) Let Z be the union of M ∖ ⋃_j V_j, the sets Φ_j(Ω_j ∖ D_j) and the exceptional sets of Step 6 (countably many Hⁿ-null sets), so Hⁿ(Z) = 0. Let x ∈ M ∖ Z. Then x ∈ V_j for some j, w := (Φ_j|Ω_j)⁻¹(x) ∈ D_j, and every weighted approximate tangent plane Q of (M, θ) at x equals T_j(w). By Step 3, G(x) ∈ Q, and by Step 4, for every v ∈ Q, HasDerivAt (fun s ↦ f (x + s • v)) ⟨G(x), v⟩ 0. Together with Steps 3 and 5 (G Borel, |G| ≤ L everywhere) this proves the statement. ∎

## Key steps

1. Cover M up to an Hⁿ-null set by countably many bi-Lipschitz C¹ charts Φ_j : Ω_j → ℝ^P (Simon Ch. 3, 1.2–1.3) and disjointify.
2. Rademacher: f∘Φ_j is differentiable Lⁿ-a.e. on Ω_j; define G on each piece as the vector in range DΦ_j representing D(f∘Φ_j), 0 elsewhere (Borel).
3. Simon Ch. 2, 4.10: ambient directional derivatives of f along range DΦ_j equal D(f∘Φ_j)u = ⟨G, DΦ_j u⟩.
4. Lipschitz bound |G| ≤ L by testing with v = G(x).
5. Weighted tangent plane of (M, θ) equals range DΦ_j a.e. on each chart (Simon Ch. 3, 1.6, 1.8(1)) and is unique since θ > 0; conclude TG(f, G).
