## Proof

Source: Step 3 and Step 5 (H5)-(H7) of the parent proof.
1. For x in V the whole segment {x + (td)e : t in [0,1]} lies in K, a subset of U; in particular x (t = 0) and x + de (t = 1) lie in K. Since U is open and u is C^1 on U, u is differentiable at every point of U.
2. (i) The translation z -> z + de is smooth and maps V into U (step 1), so z -> u(z + de) is C^1 on V (ContDiffOn.comp); u is C^1 on V, a subset of U (ContDiffOn.mono). Hence w = d^{-1}(u(. + de) - u) is C^1 on V.
3. (ii) Fix x in V. u is differentiable at x + de, so z -> u(z + de) has derivative Du(x + de) at x (chain rule with the translation); u has derivative Du(x) at x. By linearity of the derivative, w has derivative d^{-1}(Du(x + de) - Du(x)) at x, so Dw(x) = d^{-1}(Du(x + de) - Du(x)).
4. (iii) Fix x in V and let h(t) := u(x + (td)e). For t in [0,1], x + (td)e lies in K, a subset of U, so h has derivative h'(t) = Du(x + (td)e)[de] with ||h'(t)|| <= ||Du(x + (td)e)|| ||de|| <= eps |d| ||e|| (multiplying ||Du|| <= eps by ||de|| >= 0). By the mean value inequality on [0,1] (norm_image_sub_le_of_norm_deriv_le_segment_01'), ||u(x + de) - u(x)|| = ||h(1) - h(0)|| <= eps |d| ||e||. Hence ||w(x)|| = |d|^{-1} ||u(x + de) - u(x)|| <= |d|^{-1} eps |d| ||e|| = eps ||e|| (d nonzero).
5. (iv) For x, y in V, by (ii): Dw(x) - Dw(y) = d^{-1}[(Du(x + de) - Du(y + de)) - (Du(x) - Du(y))]. The four points x, y, x + de, y + de lie in K (step 1) and (x + de) - (y + de) = x - y, so ||Dw(x) - Dw(y)|| <= |d|^{-1}(H||x - y||^alpha + H||x - y||^alpha) = 2|d|^{-1} H ||x - y||^alpha. QED

## Key steps

1. segment x + [0,1]de lies in K, a subset of U
2. w is C^1 on V by composition with the translation
3. Dw = d^{-1}(Du(. + de) - Du) by the chain rule
4. mean value inequality along the segment gives ||w|| <= eps||e||
5. triangle inequality with the Hoelder bound at x, y and at x + de, y + de
