## Proof

Source: the printed steps (3)–(4) in the proof of Simon 8.5.9 (step B4 of the parent proof), with the blow-ups restricted to the fixed ball B°_5(0) instead of B°_k(0); integrality of each blow-up is the sibling rescaled_integral. Notation: G := Grassmannian N n (compact); μ_V := V.weight; ‖δV‖ := variationMeasure V; B_ρ(x) := closedBall x ρ, B°_ρ(x) := ball x ρ; H^n := μHE[n]; ω_n := unitBallVolume n ∈ (0,∞); v(P) := planeVarifoldMeasure P; V'_{ξ,λ} := rescaledMeasure V' ξ λ; O := B°_5(0).

0. Preliminaries.
 (a) Transfer of the convergence. If F ∈ C_c(ℝ^N × G) and tsupport F ⊆ U × G, then ∫ F dV_i → ∫ F dV. Indeed the restriction of F to the open subspace U ×ˢ univ is continuous with compact support (tsupport F is a compact subset of U × G), so RadonConverges applies to it, and ∫ F|_{U×G} d(V_i.domainMeasure) = ∫ F dV_i.measure because domainMeasure is the comap of V_i.measure along the inclusion of the measurable set U × G and V_i.measure vanishes off U × G (likewise for V).
 (b) Rescaled integrals. For every varifold V' on U, f ∈ C_c(ℝ^N × G) and λ > 0: ∫ f dV'_{ξ,λ} = λ^{-n} ∫ f(λ^{-1}(z − ξ), S) dV'(z,S) (definition of rescaledMeasure, integral_map, integral_smul_measure).
 (c) Let c' := max{c, 1}. For 0 < ρ < r: liminf_i ‖δV_i‖(B_ρ(ξ)) ≤ ofReal(cρ^n) ≤ ofReal(c'ρ^n) < ofReal(2c'ρ^n).
 (d) Mass of V near ξ. HasTangentPlane V ξ P θ₀ gives ξ ∈ U, θ₀ > 0 and ∫ f dV_{ξ,λ} → θ₀∫ f dv(P) as λ ↓ 0 for every f ∈ C_c(ℝ^N × G). Let χ_7 : ℝ → [0,1] be continuous with χ_7 = 1 on (−∞,6] and χ_7 = 0 on [7,∞); (z,S) ↦ χ_7(|z|) lies in C_c(ℝ^N × G). Hence λ^{-n}∫ χ_7(|z − ξ|/λ) dμ_V(z) → θ₀∫_{P.plane} χ_7(|y|) dH^n(y) ≤ θ₀H^n(P.plane ∩ B_7(0)) = θ₀ω_n7^n (H^n(P.plane ∩ B_s(0)) = ω_n s^n: P.plane is isometric to ℝ^n, on which μHE[n] is Lebesgue measure; Isometry.euclideanHausdorffMeasure_image, EuclideanSpace.euclideanHausdorffMeasure_eq_volume). As 1_{B_{6λ}(ξ)}(z) ≤ χ_7(|z − ξ|/λ), there is λ̄ > 0 with μ_V(B_{6λ}(ξ)) ≤ (θ₀ω_n7^n + 1)λ^n for 0 < λ < λ̄.

1. A countable dense family. Let 𝓕 := {f ∈ C_c(ℝ^N × G) : tsupport f ⊆ O × G}. There are f_m ∈ 𝓕 (m ∈ ℕ) such that every f ∈ 𝓕 is a uniform limit of functions from {f_m}. For instance: for each integer R ≥ 1 fix a continuous ψ_R : ℝ^N → [0,1] equal to 1 on B_{5−1/R}(0) and to 0 outside B°_{5−1/(2R)}(0), and take all products ψ_R(z)·π(z,S) with π a polynomial with rational coefficients in the coordinates of z and the matrix entries of S. This family is countable and contained in 𝓕. If f ∈ 𝓕, then tsupport f ⊆ B_{5−1/R}(0) × G for some R (compactness); by Stone–Weierstrass on the compact set B_{5−1/(2R)}(0) × G (polynomials in the coordinates of z and the entries of S.1 form a point-separating subalgebra containing constants) there are real polynomials converging uniformly to f there, and replacing their coefficients by nearby rationals keeps uniform convergence on this compact set; so there are rational polynomials π_l → f uniformly on B_{5−1/(2R)}(0) × G, whence ψ_R π_l → ψ_R f = f uniformly on ℝ^N × G (both sides vanish off B°_{5−1/(2R)}(0) × G).

2. Choice of scales. For each integer k ≥ 1 choose λ_k > 0 with (i) λ_k ≤ 1/k; (ii) 6λ_k < r and B_{7λ_k}(ξ) ⊆ U (U is open and ξ ∈ U); (iii) λ_k < λ̄; (iv) |∫ f_m dV_{ξ,λ_k} − θ₀∫ f_m dv(P)| < 1/k for all m ≤ k (finitely many conditions, each valid for all small λ by HasTangentPlane V ξ P θ₀).

3. Choice of indices. Put i_0 := 0. For k ≥ 1 choose i_k > i_{k−1} such that (v) ‖δV_{i_k}‖(B_{5λ_k}(ξ)) ≤ ofReal(2c'(5λ_k)^n); (vi) |∫ f_m d(V_{i_k})_{ξ,λ_k} − ∫ f_m dV_{ξ,λ_k}| < 1/k for all m ≤ k; (vii) μ_{V_{i_k}}(B_{5λ_k}(ξ)) ≤ (θ₀ω_n7^n + 2)λ_k^n. This is possible: (v) holds for infinitely many i by 0(c), as 5λ_k < r and a liminf strictly below a value is frequently below it; (vi) holds for all large i, because by 0(b) ∫ f_m dV'_{ξ,λ_k} = λ_k^{-n}∫ F_{m,k} dV' with F_{m,k}(z,S) := f_m(λ_k^{-1}(z − ξ), S) ∈ C_c(ℝ^N × G), tsupport F_{m,k} ⊆ B°_{5λ_k}(ξ) × G ⊆ U × G, and 0(a) applies; (vii) holds for all large i: with χ' : ℝ → [0,1] continuous, χ' = 1 on (−∞,5], χ' = 0 on [6,∞), and F_k(z,S) := χ'(|z − ξ|/λ_k) ∈ C_c(ℝ^N × G), tsupport F_k ⊆ B_{6λ_k}(ξ) × G ⊆ U × G, we have μ_{V_i}(B_{5λ_k}(ξ)) ≤ ∫ F_k dV_i → ∫ F_k dV ≤ μ_V(B_{6λ_k}(ξ)) ≤ (θ₀ω_n7^n + 1)λ_k^n by 0(a), 0(d) and (iii).

4. The varifolds. For k ≥ 1 let m_k := ((V_{i_k})_{ξ,λ_k}).restrict (O × G). This is the measure of an n-varifold W_k on O: O is open; m_k((ℝ^N ∖ O) × G) = 0; and for compact K ⊆ O, m_k(K × G) ≤ λ_k^{-n} V_{i_k}(σ_k(K) × G) < ∞, where σ_k(y) := ξ + λ_k y, because the preimage of K × G under (z,S) ↦ (λ_k^{-1}(z − ξ), S) is σ_k(K) × G and σ_k(K) is a compact subset of B°_{5λ_k}(ξ) ⊆ U (finite_on_compact of V_{i_k}). Put W_0 := W_1.

5. Integrality. For y ∈ O, ξ + λ_k y ∈ B°_{5λ_k}(ξ) ⊆ U. The sibling rescaled_integral, applied to U, O, V := V_{i_k} (integral), W := W_k, ξ and t := λ_k > 0 (W_k.measure = (rescaledMeasure V_{i_k} ξ λ_k).restrict (O × G) by construction), shows that W_k is integral.

6. First-variation scaling (printed (4)). Let X be a test field on O (Varifold.TestVectorField O) and Y(z) := λ_k X(λ_k^{-1}(z − ξ)). Y is C¹ with tsupport Y = σ_k(tsupport X), a compact subset of B°_{5λ_k}(ξ) ⊆ U, so Y is a test field on U; DY(z) = DX(λ_k^{-1}(z − ξ)), hence div_S Y(z) = (div_S X)(λ_k^{-1}(z − ξ)) for every S ∈ G (tangentialDivergence only involves the derivative). By 0(b), and because div_S X vanishes off tsupport X ⊆ O (so the restriction to O × G is immaterial): δW_k(X) = ∫ div_S X dm_k = λ_k^{-n} ∫ div_S Y(z) dV_{i_k}(z,S) = λ_k^{-n} δV_{i_k}(Y). Since V_{i_k} has locally bounded first variation, ‖δV_{i_k}‖ = variationMeasure V_{i_k} = firstVariationRieszMeasure, and the polar representation (SimonGMT Varifold.isFirstVariationPolarRepresentation_riesz: δV_{i_k}(Y) = −∫⟨ν, Y⟩ d‖δV_{i_k}‖ with |ν| = 1 ‖δV_{i_k}‖-a.e.) gives |δV_{i_k}(Y)| ≤ ∫ |Y| d‖δV_{i_k}‖ ≤ λ_k (sup|X|) ‖δV_{i_k}‖(B_{5λ_k}(ξ)) ≤ λ_k (sup|X|) 2c'5^nλ_k^n by (v). Hence |δW_k(X)| ≤ 2c'5^n λ_k sup|X| for every test field X on O, wherever its support lies in O (this uniformity is what controls ‖δW_k‖ on the whole domain O).
 (a) For every open O' with compact closure in O, firstVariationNormOn W_k O' ≤ 2c'5^nλ_k < ∞ (the supremum is over test fields with |X| ≤ 1); so W_k has locally bounded first variation.
 (b) For j ≥ 1, O_j := B°_{5−1/j}(0) is open with compact closure in O, so ‖δW_k‖(O_j) = firstVariationNormOn W_k O_j ≤ 2c'5^nλ_k (SimonGMT Varifold.firstVariationRieszMeasure_eq_norm); as O_j ↑ O, ‖δW_k‖(O) = sup_j ‖δW_k‖(O_j) ≤ 2c'5^nλ_k ≤ 2c'5^n/k. Hence ‖δW_k‖(B°_5(0)) → 0.

7. Convergence (printed (3)). For m ≤ k, since f_m vanishes off O × G, ∫ f_m dW_k = ∫ f_m d(V_{i_k})_{ξ,λ_k}, so by (iv) and (vi) |∫ f_m dW_k − θ₀∫ f_m dv(P)| < 2/k; hence ∫ f_m dW_k → θ₀∫ f_m dv(P) for every m. Uniform mass bound: μ_{W_k}(O) = m_k(O × G) = λ_k^{-n}μ_{V_{i_k}}(B°_{5λ_k}(ξ)) ≤ θ₀ω_n7^n + 2 =: C_M by (vii), and θ₀·v(P)(O × G) = θ₀H^n(P.plane ∩ O) ≤ θ₀ω_n5^n ≤ C_M. Let f ∈ 𝓕 and η > 0, and choose m with sup|f − f_m| < η (step 1). As f − f_m vanishes off O × G, |∫(f − f_m) dW_k| ≤ ηC_M and θ₀|∫(f − f_m) dv(P)| ≤ ηC_M, so limsup_k |∫ f dW_k − θ₀∫ f dv(P)| ≤ 2ηC_M. As η > 0 is arbitrary, ∫ f dW_k → θ₀∫ f dv(P).

Steps 4–7 give n-varifolds W_k on B°_5(0) that are integral, have locally bounded first variation, satisfy ‖δW_k‖(B°_5(0)) → 0, and satisfy ∫ f dW_k → θ₀∫ f dv(P) for every f ∈ C_c(ℝ^N × G) with tsupport f ⊆ B°_5(0) × G. ∎

## Key steps

1. Transfer RadonConverges to ambient C_c test functions supported in U × G; rescaled integrals; c' = max(c,1); mass bound μ_V(B_{6λ}(ξ)) ≤ (θ₀ω_n7^n+1)λ^n from the tangent plane.
2. Countable sup-norm dense family {f_m} in {f ∈ C_c : tsupport f ⊆ B°_5(0) × G} (Stone–Weierstrass with cut-offs).
3. Choose λ_k ↓ 0 (blow-up accuracy for f_m, m ≤ k) and then i_k ↑ with ‖δV_{i_k}‖(B_{5λ_k}(ξ)) ≤ 2c'(5λ_k)^n, closeness of rescaled integrals and mass bounds.
4. W_k := (V_{i_k})_{ξ,λ_k} ⌞ (B°_5(0) × G) is a varifold on B°_5(0), integral by the sibling rescaled_integral.
5. First-variation scaling δW_k(X) = λ_k^{-n}δV_{i_k}(λ_kX(·/λ_k)) and the polar representation give ‖δW_k‖(B°_5(0)) ≤ 2c'5^nλ_k → 0 and LBFV.
6. Convergence on {f_m} plus the uniform mass bound give ∫ f dW_k → θ₀∫ f dv(P) for all admissible f.
